Notes: the PWA home deliberately has no search bar — the phone browser owns search; the app's home is quick actions + recents + folders. Icons are a hand-authored inline SVG sprite (stroke, currentColor) plus the real logo SVG — no emoji, no external files, offline-safe. Everything else reuses shipping pwa/app.css classes verbatim (.topbar, .row, .row.note, .notebook, .sheet, .inbox-fab, .note-page, .note-task-box with data-line). New pieces are only: .quick-grid/.action-tile (same component as the PC mockup, phone-sized), .section-header (generalized from .notes-section-header), .skeleton, and .scrim under sheets.
