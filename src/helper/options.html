Connect your server

The TabPaladin web app at your chosen address will be able to see the addresses and titles of your open tabs and to open and close tabs. The server operator can see what the web app receives. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Signing out disconnects tab features. Reconnect here to use them again. Changing the address keeps queues and old data for their original server.
